Products

What We Produce

From industrial-grade dried cassava chips to high-value derivatives — building a complete cassava value chain.

Current Product

Dried Cassava Chips

Our primary product is high-quality dried cassava chips produced to meet industrial standards for starch, ethanol, and animal feed processors.

  • Consistent moisture content and size
  • Suitable for industrial processing
  • Reliable supply from farm to factory
  • Available for local and export markets
Future Products

Expanding the Cassava Value Chain

Beyond dried cassava chips, we are developing high-value industrial derivatives for local and export markets.

01

Industrial Ethanol

Feedstock for fuel blending and industrial solvent use.

02

Cassava Starch

High-quality starch for food, paper, and textile industries.

03

Glucose Syrup

A key sweetening and processing ingredient for food manufacturers.

04

Cassava Flour

A gluten-free flour for food production and export.

05

Acetone

An industrial solvent for manufacturing and pharmaceutical use.

06

Butanol

Used as an industrial solvent and biofuel additive.
