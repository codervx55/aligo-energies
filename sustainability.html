Growing prosperity sustainably

Sustainability is central to everything we do. We are committed to long-term economic, environmental and social value through responsible industrial development.

Our commitments

Responsible by design

Supporting local cassava farmers

Fair, stable offtake for cassava farmers in the Ogun–Oyo belt.

Creating quality employment

Dependable, skilled jobs in the local community.

Promoting renewable energy solutions

Cleaner industrial energy solutions across operations.

Reducing emissions through CNG

CNG-powered operations lower our environmental footprint.

Efficient water recycling systems

Efficient water recycling across the refinery process.

Responsible waste management

Responsible handling and reuse of processing by-products.

Community impact

The refinery is expected to:

  • Create 300–500 jobs
  • Strengthen rural livelihoods
  • Improve farmer incomes
  • Encourage youth participation in agriculture
  • Expand Nigeria's agro-industrial exports

Turning cassava into industrial products for manufacturing, food, energy and export markets.
