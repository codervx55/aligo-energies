From farm to factory

Aligo Energies Ltd is a Nigerian agro-industrial company committed to advancing industrial cassava processing through innovation, sustainability and strategic partnerships.

Building on years of practical experience in cassava sourcing and agricultural value chain operations, Aligo Energies was established to bridge the gap between agricultural production and industrial demand. We are developing a modern cassava refinery that can supply standardised products to local industries and export markets.

Our story

Our journey began through the operations of Aligo Global Ventures and Melizbode Global Ventures. There we built extensive experience supplying cassava tubers, dried cassava chips and starch to major industrial buyers across Nigeria.

We saw growing demand for industrial cassava derivatives and a shortage of local processing capacity. So we established Aligo Energies Ltd to build a fully integrated cassava refinery that creates lasting value from farm to factory.

Vision

To become a leading integrated cassava processing and agrienergy company delivering sustainable industrial solutions across Africa and global markets.

Mission

To develop scalable, technology-driven cassava processing infrastructure that turns agricultural produce into high-value industrial products, while empowering farming communities and strengthening supply chains.
