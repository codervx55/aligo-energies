    Welcome to Aligo Energies

    Aligo Energies Ltd is an indigenous Nigerian agro-industrial company developing the Aligo Energies Cassava Refinery Complex in Abúlé Àrè, Alamala, Abeokuta, Ogun State.

    Our mission is to transform cassava into high-value industrial products that support manufacturing, food production, renewable energy and export markets, while creating sustainable opportunities for farmers, communities and investors.

    The refinery complex is being developed in phases. It begins with industrial dried cassava chips and then expands into ethanol, starch, glucose syrup, cassava flour and other value-added derivatives.
