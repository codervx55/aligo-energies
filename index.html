Who We Are

Rooted in agriculture.
Committed to industrial excellence.

Aligo Energies Ltd is an indigenous Nigerian agro-industrial company advancing industrial cassava processing through innovation, sustainability and strategic partnerships.

Our mission is to transform cassava into high-value industrial products that support manufacturing, food production, renewable energy and export markets — while creating lasting opportunities for farmers, communities and investors.
