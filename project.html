Our flagship agro-industrial project

From cassava harvest to industrial value.

The Aligo Energies Cassava Refinery Complex is a phased development designed to convert cassava into dependable industrial raw materials for food, manufacturing, pharmaceutical and renewable-energy markets.

Development roadmap

A phased path to full-scale refining.

The project is structured to establish a core production operation first, then expand into a broader range of cassava derivatives as infrastructure and operating capacity develop.

01 / Foundation
Phase One
2026–2028

Industrial Dried Cassava Chips Production Facility

The foundation phase aims to establish a standardized supply of industrial-grade dried cassava chips for ethanol producers, animal-feed manufacturers and export markets.

220 MT Fresh cassava daily processing target
~16,200 MT Annual production target stated for the phase
Ethanol producers Animal-feed manufacturers Export markets
02 / Expansion
Phase Two
Post-2028

Expansion into higher-value derivatives

Building on Phase One’s infrastructure and operating experience, the next stage is planned to broaden the product portfolio into additional industrial and export opportunities.

Industrial ethanol Cassava starch Glucose syrup Cassava flour Acetone Butanol
Phase Two is presented as a future expansion stage; product mix, timing and capacity will depend on project development and investment decisions.
Project highlights

Designed for the realities of local production.

Our development approach brings together access to raw materials, energy planning and a scalable industrial setup.

01

Strategic location

Positioned within the Ogun–Oyo cassava belt to support access to a major raw-material growing area.

02

Energy-conscious operations

CNG-powered operations are part of the project’s approach to managing energy use, emissions and processing costs.

03

OEM equipment sourcing

Equipment sourcing directly from original equipment manufacturers is intended to support fit-for-purpose plant infrastructure.

04

Scalable infrastructure

The phased plan is designed to create a foundation for expanding from dried chips into a wider range of derivatives.
